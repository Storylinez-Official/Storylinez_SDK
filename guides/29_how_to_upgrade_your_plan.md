## Understanding Storylinez Plan Tiers

### Starter Plan
**Designed for**: Individual creators and small businesses starting with AI video content

**Key Features:**
- Limited monthly video generations
- Basic AI voices and templates
- Standard video resolution (1080p)
- Email support
- Basic analytics and reporting
- Standard rendering speed

**Limitations:**
- Monthly usage caps
- Limited customization options
- Basic brand integration

- Team collaboration tools

- API access for integrations

**Enhanced Capabilities:**
- Advanced editing features
- Custom voice training options
- Extended storage capacity
- Multiple organization support

- Unlimited or very high video generation limits

**Enterprise Benefits:**

## Step-by-Step Upgrade Process

### Accessing Plan Management
1. **Navigate to Settings**
   - Log into your Storylinez account at https://app.storylinezads.com
   - Click on your profile or organization settings
   - Select "Billing" or "Plan Management"
   - Review your current plan details

2. **Current Plan Review**
   - Check your current usage statistics
   - Review remaining allowances and limits
   - Identify any usage warnings or limitations
   - Note your current billing cycle and renewal date

### Comparing Plan Options
1. **Feature Comparison**
   - Review detailed feature matrices for each plan
   - Compare monthly limits and allowances
   - Evaluate advanced features and capabilities
   - Consider future needs and growth requirements

2. **Cost Analysis**
   - Compare monthly and annual pricing options
   - Calculate cost per video for different plans
   - Consider total cost of ownership
   - Factor in potential ROI from upgraded features

### Initiating the Upgrade
1. **Plan Selection**
   - Choose the plan that best fits your needs
   - Select monthly or annual billing (annual often includes discounts)
   - Review the features included in your selected plan
   - Confirm upgrade timing and effective date

2. **Payment Configuration**
   - Add or update payment method
   - Verify billing information and address
   - Set up automatic renewal preferences
   - Configure invoice and receipt delivery

3. **Upgrade Confirmation**
   - Review upgrade summary and costs
   - Confirm billing cycle and renewal settings
   - Complete the upgrade process
   - Receive confirmation and receipt

## Understanding Workflow Dependencies

### Cascade Outdated Markers
Plan upgrades don't typically trigger cascade outdated markers, but new features may require updates to existing projects.

**When upgrading:**
1. Existing projects continue working with previous capabilities
2. New features become available for new projects
3. Existing projects can be updated to use new features
4. No workflow disruption during upgrade process

### Plan-Specific Features
Some features are only available in higher-tier plans:
- **Advanced AI models**: May require project regeneration to utilize
- **4K rendering**: Existing projects can be re-rendered in higher quality
- **Premium voices**: Available for new voiceover generation
- **Advanced analytics**: Historical data available immediately

## Troubleshooting Common Issues

### Billing Problems
1. **Payment Failures**
   - Verify payment method validity and limits
   - Check billing address and information accuracy
   - Contact bank for potential blocks or restrictions
   - Update payment method if necessary

2. **Unexpected Charges**
   - Review billing history and usage reports
   - Check for overage charges or additional features
   - Verify plan details and included allowances
   - Contact support for billing clarification

### Feature Access Issues
1. **New Features Not Available**
   - Verify upgrade has been processed completely
   - Check account permissions and roles
   - Clear browser cache and refresh interface
   - Contact support if features remain unavailable

2. **Team Access Problems**
   - Verify team member invitations and acceptances
   - Check organization settings and permissions
   - Ensure users are assigned to correct organizations
   - Update user roles as needed for new features

## Plan Downgrade Considerations

### When to Consider Downgrading
1. **Reduced Usage**
   - Significant decrease in video creation needs
   - Team size reduction or restructuring
   - Budget constraints or cost optimization requirements
   - Change in business priorities or focus

2. **Feature Redundancy**
   - Advanced features no longer needed
   - Alternative solutions implemented
   - Simplified workflow requirements
   - Cost-benefit analysis favoring lower tier

### Downgrade Process
1. **Feature Impact Analysis**
   - Identify features that will be lost
   - Plan alternative solutions for lost capabilities
   - Backup any plan-specific data or configurations
   - Communicate changes to affected team members

2. **Timing Considerations**
   - Plan downgrade for end of billing cycle
   - Use remaining allowances before downgrade
   - Complete any work requiring higher-tier features
   - Update team workflows and processes
