## Understanding Organization Structure

### Organization Hierarchy
Storylinez organizations follow this structure:
- **Organization Owner**: Full administrative access and billing control
- **Administrators**: Manage members, settings, and organizational configuration
- **Managers**: Oversee specific teams or projects with limited admin access
- **Members**: Standard users with content creation and collaboration access
- **Viewers**: Read-only access for stakeholders and reviewers

### Permission Levels
1. **Full Access**: Complete organization control including billing and settings
2. **Administrative**: Member management and organizational configuration
3. **Project Management**: Project creation, assignment, and team coordination
4. **Content Creation**: Video creation, editing, and publishing capabilities
5. **Review Only**: View projects and provide feedback without editing access

## Accessing Member Management

### Navigation to Member Settings
1. **Organization Dashboard**
   - Log into your Storylinez account at https://app.storylinezads.com
   - Ensure you're in the correct organization using the organization switcher
   - Navigate to Organization Settings or Team Management
   - Select "Members" or "Team" from the settings menu

2. **Permission Verification**
   - Verify you have member management permissions
   - Check your current role and access level
   - Confirm you can view existing team members
   - Ensure you can access invitation features

### Current Team Overview
- **Member List**: View all current organization members
- **Role Summary**: See assigned roles and permission levels
- **Activity Status**: Check member login and activity patterns
- **Project Assignments**: Review member project associations

## Step-by-Step Member Invitation Process

### Preparing for Invitations
1. **Role Definition**
   - Determine appropriate role for each new member
   - Consider their responsibilities and access needs
   - Plan their project assignments and workflow integration
   - Prepare onboarding materials and documentation

2. **Information Gathering**
   - Collect accurate email addresses for invitations
   - Verify new members understand their role expectations
   - Confirm they have necessary technology and access
   - Prepare any required training or setup materials

### Sending Invitations
1. **Access Invitation Interface**
   - Click "Invite Members" or "Add Team Member" button
   - Enter new member email addresses
   - Select appropriate role and permission level
   - Add personal invitation message if desired

2. **Role Assignment**
   - **Administrator**: For team leads who need member management access
   - **Manager**: For project managers overseeing specific initiatives
   - **Member**: For content creators and collaborators
   - **Viewer**: For stakeholders who need review access only

3. **Custom Permissions** (if available in your plan)
   - Configure specific feature access
   - Set project-level permissions
   - Define workflow participation rights
   - Establish content approval authority

### Invitation Configuration
1. **Access Controls**
   - Set initial project assignments
   - Configure folder and content access
   - Define collaboration permissions
   - Establish review and approval rights

2. **Notification Settings**
   - Configure email notification preferences
   - Set up project update alerts
   - Define communication channels
   - Establish escalation procedures

## Role and Permission Management

### Detailed Role Descriptions

#### Administrator Role
**Capabilities:**
- Invite and remove organization members
- Manage roles and permissions for all users
- Configure organization settings and preferences
- Access billing and subscription management
- Oversee all projects and content within organization
- Manage integrations and API access

**Best For:**
- Team leads and department managers
- IT administrators and system managers
- Business owners and executives

#### Manager Role
**Capabilities:**
- Create and manage assigned projects
- Coordinate team workflows and assignments
- Review and approve content within their domain
- Access advanced analytics and reporting
- Manage project timelines and deliverables
- Configure project-specific settings

**Best For:**
- Project managers and team coordinators
- Department supervisors
- Client relationship managers

#### Member Role
**Capabilities:**
- Create and edit video content
- Collaborate on assigned projects
- Access shared resources and templates
- Participate in review and feedback processes
- Use all content creation features
- Contribute to team workflows

**Best For:**
- Content creators and video producers
- Marketing specialists
- Creative team members

#### Viewer Role
**Capabilities:**
- View completed and in-progress projects
- Provide feedback and comments
- Access shared content and reports
- Participate in review processes
- Download approved content

**Best For:**
- Stakeholders and executives
- External clients and partners
- Quality assurance reviewers

## Understanding Workflow Dependencies

### Team Workflow Integration
Member additions don't typically trigger cascade outdated markers, but team changes may affect project workflows.

**When adding members:**
1. Existing projects continue with current team assignments
2. New members can be added to ongoing projects as needed
3. Permission changes apply immediately to all relevant content
4. No workflow disruption during member addition process

### Collaborative Dependencies
Team member roles affect project workflow:
- **Project Creators**: Can modify all upstream workflow elements
- **Content Editors**: Can work within assigned project areas
- **Reviewers**: Provide feedback without modifying core content
- **Viewers**: Access completed work without editing capabilities

2. **Account Creation Problems**

### Permission and Access Issues

2. **Workflow Integration Problems**
