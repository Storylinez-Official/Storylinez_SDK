# How to Switch Organizations in Project Dashboard

## Overview
This tutorial explains how to switch between different organizations once you're inside the project dashboard in Storylinez. This is essential for users who are part of multiple organizations and need to switch contexts for different projects.

## Prerequisites
- You must be logged into your Storylinez account
- You must be a member of at least one organization
- You must have access to the project dashboard

## Step-by-Step Guide

### 1. Accessing the Organization Switcher
1. Navigate to your project dashboard (after logging in)
2. Look for the organization selector in the top navigation bar
3. The current organization name will be displayed with a dropdown arrow
4. Click on the organization name to open the organization switcher

### 2. Viewing Available Organizations
1. When you click the organization switcher, a dropdown menu will appear
2. You'll see a list of all organizations you're a member of
3. Each organization entry shows:
   - Organization name
   - Your role in that organization (Owner, Admin, or Member)
   - Organization icon (if set)

### 3. Switching Organizations
1. From the dropdown menu, click on the organization you want to switch to
2. The page will reload/update to show content for the selected organization
3. The organization name in the top bar will update to reflect your selection
4. All projects, settings, and data will now be filtered to the selected organization

### 4. Verifying the Switch
1. Check that the organization name in the top bar has changed
2. Verify that the projects list shows projects from the correct organization
3. Any organization-specific settings will now reflect the selected organization

## Important Notes
- Switching organizations changes the context for all subsequent actions
- Projects, API keys, and billing are organization-specific
- Your role and permissions may differ between organizations
- Some features may be available in one organization but not another based on subscription plans

## Troubleshooting
- If you don't see the organization switcher, you may only be a member of one organization
- If an organization doesn't appear in the list, check with the organization owner about your membership status
- If switching fails, try refreshing the page and attempting again

## Related Features
- Creating new organizations: Use the "Create Organization" button in your organizations list
- Managing organization settings: Available through the organization dashboard
- Inviting team members: Done through the organization members section

This organization switching feature allows you to efficiently work across multiple teams and projects while keeping everything properly organized and secure.
