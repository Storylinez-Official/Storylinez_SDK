## Understanding Organization Structure

### Organization Hierarchy in Storylinez
- **Organization Level**: Top-level entity containing all teams, projects, and settings
- **Department/Team Level**: Sub-groups within the organization for specific functions
- **Project Level**: Individual video creation initiatives and campaigns
- **User Level**: Individual team members with specific roles and permissions

### Key Information Categories
1. **Business Profile**: Company name, description, industry, and basic information
2. **Contact Information**: Address, phone, email, and communication preferences
3. **Billing Details**: Payment methods, subscription information, and financial data
4. **Team Structure**: Departments, roles, and organizational hierarchy
5. **Brand Information**: Logo, colors, guidelines, and brand assets
6. **Security Settings**: Access controls, compliance requirements, and data protection

## Accessing Organization Settings

### Navigation to Organization Management
1. **Organization Dashboard**
   - Log into your Storylinez account at https://app.storylinezads.com
   - Use the organization switcher to select your organization
   - Navigate to Organization Settings or Admin Panel
   - Verify you have administrative permissions

2. **Settings Overview**
   - Review current organization configuration
   - Identify incomplete or outdated information
   - Plan updates and improvements needed
   - Prepare required documentation and assets

## Setting Up Business Profile

### Company Information
1. **Basic Business Details**
   - **Organization Name**: Official legal name and any doing-business-as names
   - **Industry Classification**: Select appropriate industry category for your business
   - **Business Type**: Corporation, LLC, partnership, nonprofit, etc.
   - **Company Size**: Number of employees and organizational scale

2. **Business Description**
   - **Mission Statement**: Clear description of your organization's purpose and goals
   - **Value Proposition**: What makes your organization unique and valuable
   - **Target Market**: Primary customers and audience demographics
   - **Business Focus**: Main products, services, or areas of expertise

3. **Company Culture and Values**
   - **Core Values**: Fundamental principles that guide your organization
   - **Brand Personality**: How your organization presents itself to the world
   - **Communication Style**: Formal, casual, professional, friendly, etc.
   - **Quality Standards**: Expectations for content and professional presentation

### Contact and Location Information
1. **Primary Business Address**
   - Complete street address with postal code
   - City, state/province, and country
   - Time zone configuration for scheduling and coordination
   - Regional preferences for date, time, and number formats

2. **Communication Contacts**
   - **Primary Contact**: Main point of contact for organizational matters
   - **Billing Contact**: Responsible for financial and subscription management
   - **Technical Contact**: IT or technical support coordination
   - **Support Contact**: Customer service and user support management

3. **Social Media and Web Presence**
   - **Website URL**: Primary business website address
   - **Social Media Profiles**: Links to official business social media accounts
   - **Professional Profiles**: LinkedIn, industry directories, etc.
   - **Press and Media Contacts**: For external communication and partnerships

## Understanding Workflow Dependencies

### Organization-Level Dependencies
Organization information affects all aspects of Storylinez usage:

**Global Impact:**
- **All Projects**: Organization settings apply to every project within the organization
- **Team Workflows**: Organizational structure influences all collaborative processes
- **Brand Application**: Organization brand settings affect all content creation
- **Security Policies**: Access controls and security settings apply organization-wide

**Information Updates:**
When you update organization information, changes typically apply immediately to new projects and activities, while existing projects may retain their original settings unless specifically updated.
