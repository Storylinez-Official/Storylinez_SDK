## Understanding Company Presets

### What Are Company Presets?
Company presets are comprehensive business information profiles that include:
- **Company Information**: Name, type/industry, tagline, and business description
- **Vision and Mission**: Company vision statement and core values
- **Products and Services**: Description of what your company offers
- **Contact Information**: Website links, call-to-action text and messaging
- **Business Context**: Industry-specific information and company positioning
- **Default Settings**: Standardized business information for consistent use across projects

Note: Company presets contain business information, while brand presets contain visual elements like colors, fonts, and logos. These work together to provide complete brand and business context for your content.

### Benefits of Company Presets
1. **Business Consistency**: Ensure all content includes accurate company information
2. **Context Efficiency**: Reduce setup time by providing business context automatically
3. **Information Accuracy**: Maintain consistent company details across all content
4. **Team Alignment**: Provide clear business information for all content creators
5. **Professional Standards**: Support growth while maintaining business identity

## Creating Your First Company Preset

### Brand Identity Configuration

#### Logo and Visual Assets
1. **Logo Upload**
   - Upload primary logo in high resolution (PNG with transparency preferred)
   - Add secondary/alternative logo versions if applicable
   - Configure logo placement preferences and minimum size requirements
   - Set up logo usage guidelines for different content types

2. **Brand Colors**
   - **Primary Colors**: Main brand colors with exact hex codes
   - **Secondary Colors**: Supporting palette for variety and hierarchy
   - **Accent Colors**: Highlight colors for calls-to-action and emphasis
   - **Neutral Colors**: Grays, whites, and blacks for balance and readability

3. **Typography Settings**
   - **Primary Font**: Main brand font for headlines and important text
   - **Secondary Font**: Supporting font for body text and descriptions

## Understanding Workflow Dependencies

### Brand Preset Integration
Company presets affect multiple workflow stages:

**Dependency Chain Impact:**
- **Project Settings**: Presets applied during initial project setup
- **Import**: Brand assets and media organized according to preset guidelines
- **Prompt**: Brand voice and messaging standards influence prompt creation
- **Storyboard**: Visual style and layout preferences guide storyboard design
- **Voiceover**: Voice selection and audio standards from preset configuration
- **Edit**: Brand elements and style consistency enforced throughout editing
- **Render**: Export settings and quality standards from preset specifications

**Preset Updates:**
When you update a company preset, existing projects may show indicators that newer brand standards are available, but projects continue using their original preset version unless manually updated.
