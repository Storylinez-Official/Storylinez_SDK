# How to Add People to Your Organization

## Overview
This tutorial explains how to invite new members to your organization in Storylinez. This feature allows team collaboration and helps you build your organization with the right people and permissions.

## Prerequisites
- You must be logged into your Storylinez account
- You must be an Owner or Admin of the organization
- You must have the email addresses of people you want to invite

## Step-by-Step Guide

### 1. Navigate to Organization Members
1. Switch to the organization you want to manage (if you're part of multiple organizations)
2. Go to your organization dashboard
3. Click on the "Members" tab or section
4. You'll see the current members list and an "Invite Member" button

### 2. Starting the Invitation Process
1. Click the "Invite Member" button (usually has a + or user icon)
2. An invitation dialog will open
3. The dialog will have fields for email, role, and an optional message

### 3. Filling Out the Invitation
1. **Email Address**: Enter the email address of the person you want to invite
   - Make sure the email is correct as the invitation will be sent there
   - Only one email can be invited at a time
2. **Role Selection**: Choose the appropriate role:
   - **Member**: Basic access to projects and collaboration features
   - **Admin**: Can manage other members and organization settings (but cannot delete the organization)
   - Note: Owner role cannot be assigned through invitations
3. **Optional Message**: Add a personal message to the invitation email
   - This helps the recipient understand why they're being invited
   - Keep it professional and clear

### 4. Sending the Invitation
1. Review all the information you've entered
2. Click "Send Invitation" to send the email
3. You'll see a confirmation message that the invitation was sent
4. The invitation will appear in the "Pending Invitations" section

### 5. Managing Pending Invitations
1. Switch to the "Pending Invitations" tab in the members section
2. Here you can see all invitations that haven't been accepted yet
3. For each pending invitation, you can:
   - **Resend**: Send the invitation email again
   - **Cancel**: Remove the invitation before it's accepted
4. Invitations typically expire after 7 days

### 6. What Happens Next
1. The invited person receives an email with the invitation
2. They can click a link in the email to accept or reject the invitation
3. If they accept:
   - They're automatically added to your organization
   - They gain access based on the role you assigned
   - They'll appear in your "Active Members" list
4. If they reject or ignore the invitation:
   - The invitation remains pending until it expires
   - You can resend or cancel as needed

## Understanding Roles and Permissions

### Member Role
- Can view organization details and projects
- Can collaborate on projects they're assigned to
- Cannot invite other members or change organization settings
- Can leave the organization at any time

### Admin Role
- All Member permissions
- Can invite new members and manage existing members
- Can update organization settings and branding
- Can manage organization projects and billing
- Cannot delete the organization or change the Owner's role

### Owner Role
- All Admin permissions
- Can delete the organization
- Can manage organization billing and subscriptions
- Cannot have their role changed by others
- Only one Owner per organization

## Best Practices
1. **Verify Email Addresses**: Double-check email addresses before sending invitations
2. **Choose Appropriate Roles**: Start with Member role and promote as needed
3. **Add Personal Messages**: Include context about why you're inviting them
4. **Follow Up**: Check if invitations are accepted and follow up if needed
5. **Regular Cleanup**: Cancel old pending invitations that won't be accepted

## Troubleshooting
- **Invitation Not Received**: Check spam folders, verify email address
- **Can't Send Invitation**: Ensure you have Admin or Owner permissions
- **Invitation Expired**: Send a new invitation to the same email
- **Role Restrictions**: Contact the organization Owner if you need higher permissions

## Related Features
- Removing members: Done through the Active Members list
- Changing member roles: Available for Admins and Owners
- Organization settings: Manage organization details and preferences
- Billing management: Handle subscription and payment details

Adding team members to your organization enables better collaboration and helps you scale your video creation projects effectively.
